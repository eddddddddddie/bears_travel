The estimates above come mostly from trips that do not involve Perth, such as North Queensland to Auckland and games in Darwin. The Western Reds are the only previous Perth club in the competition. 6. 2027 outlook

Perth Bears 2027 outlook

Applying the cost by distance to the distance from Perth to each opponent gives the Bears' expected travel effect in 2027. The projection assumes a balanced draw: each club plays every other club equally often, and each away game is played at the opponent's base. 7. Road trips

Two-game road trips for the Bears

The draw also affects travel: the order of away games sets how often the Bears fly between Perth and the east coast. If the Bears stay away between two consecutive away games instead of returning to Perth, the two games become one trip: Perth to the first ground, on to the second, then back to Perth. With no more than two away games in a row, the plan with the least travel is the pairing of away games that saves the most km, which can be solved exactly as a matching problem. The 2027 draw is not yet published, so the plan is computed for each of the — possible sets of away opponents.

Method

Method and limits

Results for 1995 to 2026 come from Rugby League Project, and closing lines from AusSportsBetting. Travel distance is measured from each club's base to the venue, and time-zone offsets are taken at kick-off, including daylight saving. The model uses OLS with robust standard errors on — matches. The market comparison uses — matches with a closing line.

  • Most trips over 3,000 km are between North Queensland and Auckland, or to Darwin. Few involve Perth, so the Bears' figure assumes a Perth trip costs the same as any other trip of that length.
  • The effect of a time-zone change cannot be estimated separately from distance, because nearly every time-zone crossing is a trip to or from New Zealand or Perth.
  • The 2027 projection uses a balanced draw. Neutral venues, Magic Round and any Bears home games outside Perth are not included.
  • Win chance converts points to probability for an evenly matched game, using the standard deviation of the model's residuals.
  • Distances are great-circle distances between club bases and venues. Map outlines are from Natural Earth (public domain).
